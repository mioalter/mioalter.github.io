## Pullbacks

aka implicit dependencies e.g. $f: A \to B$, $g: B \to C$, $h: B \times C \to D$,
but what is actually computed is $h\circ (f,g): A \to D$;
special case $f = id$.
So now there is an implicit dependency between the two arguments of $h$: they
are both transformations of _the same_ value.
Example with functions $\mathbb R \to \mathbb R$.
